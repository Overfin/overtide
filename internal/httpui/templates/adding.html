How adding works

Once a day, Overtide reads Overfin's suggestions: its picks, the titles your libraries lack, by value, and, by the discovery share, TMDB's trending and popular titles, so people have new things to discover. A discovered title nobody watches loses its value once it's no longer new, and removal takes it out.

Every 15 minutes it tries the titles whose turn it is, one search at a time: it adds the title to Radarr or Sonarr, tagged "overtide", searches for it, and keeps it only if a download started. Otherwise it takes it out again and tries later: after 1 day, then 2, 4, 8, 16, then every 30. Titles not out yet wait for their release. Nothing is added to a disk over its target.

Overtide manages only the titles tagged "overtide": what you add yourself is never touched.
